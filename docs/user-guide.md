# Encore user guide

How to use the Encore app. To install and run it, see the [README](../README.md#run) and [setup guide](setup.md). The app has a shorter version of this guide under **About → Help and FAQ**.

Encore has three tabs: **My List**, **Search**, and **Friends**.

## Sign in

- **Create an account:** on the sign-in screen, press **New here? Create an account**, enter a username, email, and password, and press **Create account**. If Encore says "Confirm your email with the link we sent," open that email's link, then sign in.
- **Sign in:** enter your email and password and press **Sign in**.
- **Forgot your password:** press **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and press **Send code**. Type the **Code** from the email and a **New password**, then press **Save new password**. **Send a new code** sends another if it expired; **Use a different email** starts over.
- **Sign out:** press **Sign out** at the top of My List and confirm. You'll need your email and password to sign back in.

## Find movies and add them to your list

1. Open **Search**. With nothing typed it shows **Popular right now**.
2. Type a title. If nothing matches, Search may offer a spelling under **Did you mean**.
3. Press **Mark watched** on a movie you've seen. It joins My List under **Waiting to be ranked**. **Undo** takes it back off.

If Search says live search isn't available, the movie database couldn't be reached, so it shows only movies already saved in Encore, without plot summaries. Try again in a minute.

## Rank a movie

Press **Rank** next to a movie (in Search, in My List, or on the movie's page).

1. **Pick a tier.** Encore asks "How did you feel about …?" Choose **Loved it**, **It was okay**, or **Didn't like it**. The movie is only compared with others in the same tier. Your first movie goes straight to #1.
2. **Compare.** Encore asks "Which did you enjoy more?" and shows two movies. Tap the one you liked more. A progress bar shows how many questions are left; it's usually only a few, even for a long list.
   - **Too close to call? Skip** asks about a different movie instead.
   - **Undo** goes back one answer. **Start over** begins again from the tier question.
   - **Cancel** leaves without changing your list.
3. **Save.** Encore shows where the movie lands. Nothing changes until you press **Save**.

Each ranked movie gets a score from 0.0 to 10.0. Loved movies score 6.7–10, okay ones 3.4–6.6, and disliked ones 0–3.3, higher for movies placed higher in their tier.

If Encore says "Your list changed in another session, so this ranking started over," your list was changed on another device or tab while you were ranking. Answer the questions again and save.

## Re-rank, review, or remove a movie

Tap a movie in My List to open its page. Under **Your take**:

- **Re-rank** runs the ranking again. Encore reminds you of your last tier.
- **Review** (or **Edit review**) opens the review screen. Write what you thought, choose **Who can read this**: **Friends** (only you and your friends) or **Everyone** (anyone signed in to Encore), and press **Save review**. You can write one review per movie.
- **Remove from your list** takes the movie off your list, along with its rank and your review.

The movie page also shows your friends' takes and public reviews. Press the comments button under a review to read or add comments. You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own comments.

## Friends

On the **Friends** tab, type in **Find people by username** and press **Add**. You become friends once they add you back.

- **Added you:** people who added you. Press **Add back** to become friends, or **Dismiss**.
- **Friends:** press **View** to see a friend's ranked list and reviews, or **Remove** to unfriend them.
- **Waiting for them to add you back:** people you added. **Cancel** takes the request back.

Friends can read each other's reviews marked **Friends**.

## Common problems

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Can't reach Encore. Check your connection and try again." | You're offline or the connection dropped. Reconnect and press **Retry**. |
| "Confirm your email with the link we sent, then sign in." | Open the link in the confirmation email (check spam), then sign in. |
| "Your session expired. Sign out and sign back in." | Sign out from My List and sign back in. |
| "Search isn't available right now. Try again." | The search service is down. Try again later. |
| "Your list changed in another session, so this ranking started over." | Answer the questions again and press **Save**. |

## Report a bug

1. Check [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Davidtm6/Encore/issues) to see whether it's already reported. If it is, add what you saw to that issue.
2. Otherwise, press **New issue → Bug report** and fill in the template: the steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened, your device and OS, and how serious it is. Screenshots help.

The team reviews new bugs every week. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#bugs) describes the full process.
